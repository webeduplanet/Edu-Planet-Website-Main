How we help learners progress

Purposeful teaching.
Practical support.

EduPlanet follows the CAPS curriculum, taught by qualified, experienced teachers. Classes of approximately 15–25 learners give teachers more opportunity to explain, challenge and guide.

  • Free extra classes provide additional academic help.
  • A counsellor, learner support coach and personalised assistance support individual needs.
  • Feedback helps learners recognise progress and understand the next step.

Clear expectations encourage preparation, respect and responsibility. Our aim is steady growth in both understanding and character.

Useful skills for life

Confidence with practical tools.

Coding and Robotics develop opportunities for logical thinking and problem-solving. A dedicated CAT lab supports practical computer learning.

High-school learners have access to Google Workspace and Canva Pro for communication, collaboration and creative work. Career guidance helps learners explore their interests and consider future pathways.

What’s included in your school fees for 2027?

At EduPlanet, we aim to provide exceptional value while making school life simpler, safer and more convenient for families.

Learning Resources

  • Textbooks: Provided from day one, saving parents up to R3,000 in upfront costs.
  • Learning Materials: Printed notes and educational resources. Stationery is not included.
  • Coding & Robotics: Practical technology education included.

Academic Support

  • Extra Classes: After-school academic support and revision programmes.
  • Additional Learning Support: Opportunities to strengthen understanding and improve results.

Sport & Extramurals

  • Sports Transport: Transport to school sporting fixtures.
  • Official Team Kits: Match kits provided for high school sports teams.
  • Kit Laundry: School-managed washing and drying of team kits.
