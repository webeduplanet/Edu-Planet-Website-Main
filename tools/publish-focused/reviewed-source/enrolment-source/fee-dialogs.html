What’s included in 2027 fees?

At EduPlanet, we aim to provide exceptional value while making school life simpler, safer and more convenient for families.

  • Textbooks Rental: Provided from day one, saving parents up to R3,000 in upfront costs. T&Cs apply
  • Learning & support: Printed resources, Coding & Robotics, and after-school classes and revision.
  • School sport: Transport to regular school fixtures, plus high school team kits and laundry.
  • Campus safety: 24/7 security, controlled access and CCTV.

Textbook rental terms

We source your child’s textbooks and provide them from day one. If you pay monthly, the rental cost is spread across the year.

Textbooks remain school property and must be returned in satisfactory condition. Lost or damaged books are charged to the learner’s school account.

Sports transport and team kits

Transport to regular school sporting fixtures is included. We also provide, wash and dry official match kits for high school sports teams.

Tournaments, sports tours and special trips, including overnight trips, may carry extra costs. We’ll tell families about these in advance.

Costs to budget for separately

  • Stationery. Personal stationery and grade-specific supplies.
  • Excursions and camps. Costs are communicated in advance.
  • Sports tours and special trips. Additional charges may apply and will be communicated in advance.
  • Supervised aftercare. An optional service charged separately. Reception can confirm hours, availability and fees.

For payment questions, email accounts@eduplanet.co.za.

What’s improving for 2027

  • Learner support. Two additional full-time staff members will strengthen mentoring, counselling support, discipline and sport.
  • Classroom technology. Upgraded classroom TVs and expanded CCTV monitoring support teaching and campus safety.
  • Parent communication. We’re introducing d6 Communicator to bring school notices, updates and reminders together.
