Join EduPlanet

Four steps,
start to finish.

Our accounts office will guide you through the application process. Complete your forms online or on paper, then book a visit to meet us.

2027 applications close: 30 October 2026.

Late applications will be considered on a case-by-case basis, subject to availability.

Start your application ↓
01

Complete the application form

Complete the online application or download, print and fill in the PDF form.

School fees are also available on the application form.

02

Book a school tour and learner interview

Visit the school as a family. Learners in Grade 1 and above take part in a phase-appropriate interview.

03

Receive your outcome

We review your child’s interview, academic results and behaviour information, then send an offer or an update with the next steps and feedback timeline.

04

Accept your offer and secure your child’s place

Accept the enrolment agreement and pay the registration payment by your offer’s deadline. We then send written enrolment confirmation.

Value for your family

What’s included in your school fees for 2027?

At EduPlanet, we aim to provide exceptional value while making school life simpler, safer and more convenient for families.

Learning Resources

  • Textbooks: Provided from day one, saving parents up to R3,000 in upfront costs.
  • Learning Materials: Printed notes and educational resources. Stationery is not included.
  • Coding & Robotics: Practical technology education included.

Academic Support

  • Extra Classes: After-school academic support and revision programmes.
  • Additional Learning Support: Opportunities to strengthen understanding and improve results.

Sport & Extramurals

  • Sports Transport: Transport to school sporting fixtures.
  • Official Team Kits: Match kits provided for high school sports teams.
  • Kit Laundry: School-managed washing and drying of team kits.

Safety & Security

  • 24/7 Security: Security measures to protect our campus.
  • Controlled Access: Fingerprint access control and perimeter security fencing.
  • CCTV Monitoring: Cameras throughout the campus and classrooms.
  • Supervised Aftercare: Safe and affordable supervised aftercare options available.

Less to worry about. More to look forward to.

  • Fewer unexpected expenses
  • Less paperwork and fewer separate payments
  • Greater convenience for parents
  • More opportunities for learners
  • Peace of mind in a safe, supportive environment
