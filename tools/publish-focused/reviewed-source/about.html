About

Rooted in Christ.
Growing together.

Since 2016, EduPlanet has brought learning and character development together for learners from Grade R to Grade 12.

Our community

Every child is uniquely created and deeply valued.

Meaningful relationships and purposeful teaching help learners discover their gifts, develop confidence and take responsibility. Our school community is shaped by Christian faith, a commitment to excellence and care for one another.

Our vision

The future we aspire to.

To raise up servant leaders rooted in Christ, united in love and courageous in living out their God-given calling.

Our mission

Our daily commitment.

To cultivate a joyful, Christ-centred learning community where every learner belongs, is inspired to grow and equipped to make a difference.

Through meaningful relationships, excellent education and intentional character development, we nurture each learner’s unique gifts and equip them to lead with humility, serve with courage and live with purpose.

Faith lived daily

Our foundation. Our character. Our values.

Our foundation is the Gospel of Jesus Christ. We are loved before we achieve; that assurance shapes how we learn, lead and serve.

Our Christian foundation

Righteousness
Our identity and worth are received through Christ.
Peace
We find belonging in God’s love and build relationships with care.
Joy
We approach learning and life with hope and gratitude.

The character we develop

Humility
Remain teachable, value others and learn from feedback.
Courage
Embrace challenges, take initiative and do what is right.
Discipline
Take ownership, direct attention and give consistent effort.

Our guiding values

Gratitude
Appreciate God’s goodness and the contributions of others.
Excellence
Give wholehearted effort, act with integrity and keep improving.
Service
Lead with love and use your gifts for the good of others.

Be grateful. Give your best. Serve others.

Minds, bodies and spirits

Growing through learning and participation.

Academic work, sport and creative activities give learners opportunities to develop their strengths. We encourage healthy discipline, celebrate progress and practise servant leadership through everyday acts of care.

Bible School integration, a Youth Ministry Pastor and organised learner camps support Christian pastoral development.
